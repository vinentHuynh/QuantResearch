Point-in-time contract

  • Formation RVOL uses only earlier volume from the same session-time slot.
  • BOS uses only swings confirmed before the impulse candle.
  • A continuous overlap with a zone is one touch episode.
  • Touch-candle rejection is available one minute after the touch begins.
  • MFE/MAE and 1R/2R outcomes begin after that confirmation time and are labels, never filters.
  • Contract-roll formations remain in the data but carry roll_formation=true.
