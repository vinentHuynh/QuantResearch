    The 24h book is LONG-ONLY BETA over a decade in which gold went 1,150 -> 4,600
    and equities ran hard. Its Sharpe is one bull regime, not an edge, and it carries
    the full drawdown at 0.95 x tau. The overnight block at least has an economic
    story. Weigh [4] and [6] with that in mind.
    MES/MNQ correlate ~+0.9: two equity index futures are ONE bet wearing
    two tickers, and the IDM of ~1.0 says so. The commodity legs are what
    actually diversify -- which is why they earn a place despite weak
    standalone overnight numbers.
