# Session extremes failure test: frozen research protocol

Created September 30, 2026 before inspecting scored strategy outcomes. Test two prespecified level detectors separately: previous regular-session extremes and overnight extremes. No pattern ranking or parameter optimization. NQ history has been inspected by earlier projects; every window is retrospective evidence, not an untouched holdout.

## Rules and assumptions

- Dataset: registered NQ `3b9114199a2f6f0e51b70a63edc43de00940ae5df8d047b3aaa6aa8424aac9f1-v1`, checksum `67092a9b6201747c46ae08029068fd03b42c5def68339ac2434dba78a77386c7`; 15-minute full-trading-day bars, 30 calendar warmup days.
- Previous day means the previous scheduled regular session, 08:30-15:00 America/Chicago, capped at the frozen CME index calendar close. A missing scheduled prior session is not silently replaced by an older observed session. Overnight means previous calendar day 17:00 through current 08:30 Chicago. Require all expected completed 15-minute source candles; this does not certify every source minute is present. Do not forward fill. Weekend/holiday and shortened-session logic uses the preserved calendar.
- Freeze two zones at the regular open, centered on each reference extreme with half-width 0.10 times the last completed pre-open ATR(14). ATR is a simple completed true-range average. Zones expire at that day's regular close. This is the same outside padding as the old cluster strategy; a single-price level lacks the variable gap between paired pivots.
- Preserve the existing arming/first-visit rule: arm from a completed close on the expected approach side, including the pre-open close. The first subsequent physical visit consumes the zone whether it qualifies or not. Previous close and signal open must be outside the zone on the approach side. Sweep at least one tick beyond the far edge, close inside the zone, then enter in the reversal direction at the next contiguous 15-minute open. Warmup initializes but cannot trade. One position maximum; a consumed zone cannot trade again.
- Both detectors permit new entries only during regular hours, with signal completion and next open strictly before the declared regular close. Positions retain the original 20 entry-inclusive chart-bar exit and can carry after regular close. Stop one tick beyond the sweep; cancel entry opening through stop. Target remains the original exceptional-move guard, 1,000 signal ATR away.
- One NQ contract, $100,000 starting capital, $2.50 commission and one tick slippage per contract per side. One-minute OHLC stops with the existing event engine and conservative ambiguity. No risk sizing, loss cap, news filter, matched generic-sweep control, roll adjustment or additional event entry-delay stress.
- Comparison to the old cluster campaign is descriptive: these tests introduce daily level lifetimes, regular-hour entry eligibility and a constant single-level width as well as replacing the detector.

## Frozen sequence and research gates

1. Development: 2022-01-01 through 2024-12-31, exactly one baseline per detector. Proposed gates (user specified no numeric criteria): successful reconciled run, at least 100 closed trades, positive net P&L, profit factor at least 1.05, at least two of three exit-calendar years profitable, and maximum marked drawdown at most 35%. Stop each detector independently at the first decisive failure; retain both outcomes.
2. If eligible, one Workbench chronological walk-forward fold: 366 training days covering 2024 followed by 365 test days covering 2025; one fixed candidate, net-P&L selection metric, at least 20 training trades. Baseline and doubled commission/slippage, no additional delay. Require both joined scenarios to have strictly positive P&L, profit factor above 1, at least 40 trades and marked drawdown at most 35%. Any unavailable or failed scenario blocks advancement.
3. Only after evaluation passes, test unchanged on 2026-01-01 through 2026-09-28 (latest complete calendar day before the dataset's partial tail), baseline and doubled costs. Require positive net P&L, PF above 1, at least 25 trades, drawdown at most 35% in both. Then test nearby zone half-widths 0.075 and 0.125 on development history, requiring positive P&L, PF above 1, 100 trades, two profitable years and drawdown at most 35% for each. Keep every attempted variant; stop at a failed gate.
4. No market-transfer claim and no automatic Watchlist promotion. Delay robustness and incremental location value remain untested even if all executable gates pass.

Source snapshots, previews, API run IDs, preserved protocols, complete ledgers, checksums, warnings and stage decisions will be retained in the campaign. Report technical execution success separately from economic eligibility.
