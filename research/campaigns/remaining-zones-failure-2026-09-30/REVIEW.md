# Detection review before scored launch

The deterministic December 2021 formation chart was inspected before launching the 2022-2024 screen. All ten candidates produced warmup-era formations. The first formation per candidate was used, without selecting by later profitability. Numerical source-release checks passed for every preview formation.

Ten focused detector tests passed, including completed-right-bar higher-timeframe confirmation, source-gap rejection, explicit departure/role-flip prerequisites, fixed opening windows and pivot formulas, active-level deduplication, shallow first-touch retirement, and future-price/prefix causality for all ten modes. The production build passed with its bundle-size advisory.

The zones remain narrow and the close must finish inside the zone; a stronger reclaim past the near edge is excluded. This limitation is accepted for this common initial screen so the trigger is not changed after observing results. Higher-timeframe confirmation can activate a distant level long after its original extreme; no discretionary significance or distance filter is added.

The previous-bar benchmark is descriptive, not a matched control. The study does not claim that differences in raw P&L isolate the incremental value of the level detector.
