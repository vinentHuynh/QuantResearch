# Supply/demand zone-quality research

Completed 48 new fixed-rule historical simulations across MNQ, MGC, ES and CL, testing width, departure strength, formation volume, elapsed age, opposing room, hourly direction and strict FVG. All cases and prior research remain visible.

**No added factor cleared the declared evidence requirement.** Room and hourly context have useful descriptive support, while narrow zones, stronger departure, higher volume and young age are not reliable universal requirements. Fixed-contract dollar profits and average return per unit of risk can disagree.
